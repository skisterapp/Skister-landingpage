# Skister — project brief

Skister is the easiest way to share ski gear with people you trust. Winter sports remain the primary focus; camping, hiking, climbing and other outdoor equipment are supported as secondary capabilities within trusted networks (friends, family, clubs and communities). This repository includes the marketing/landing site (static HTML), blog, and related tooling.

## Operational notes

- Canonical contact emails and landing-page git workflow are documented in `memory-bank/activeContext.md`.
- Mobile app / Edge Functions live in the sibling repo `Skisterapp`.
