Delete your Skister account

This page explains how to delete a Skister account and what happens to your data. Skister is the ski equipment sharing app by Skister (com.skister.app).

How to delete your account in the app

  1. Open the Skister app and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile → Settings → Account.
  3. Choose Delete Account.
  4. Confirm by typing DELETE when asked.
  5. You may need to have signed in recently (re-authentication) for security.

Account deletion is self-service in the app. After a successful deletion you are signed out and cannot use that account again.

What is deleted

  • Your login (auth) account
  • Profile details (name, username, bio, phone, avatar, location preferences)
  • Your gear listings and related photos/files you uploaded
  • Device push tokens and in-app notifications for your account
  • Invitations, follows/blocks, and similar network links tied to your account
  • Private tool calculation history and similar per-user records
  • Subscription records we store for Premium entitlement (store billing remains with Apple/Google)

What may be retained or anonymised

  • Messages you sent may show as deleted/anonymised for the other participant rather than removing their entire conversation history.
  • Rental/reservation history shared with another user may be kept in anonymised form (for example displaying “Deleted user”) so the other party’s records stay consistent.
  • We may retain information when required by law or for abuse/security investigations.

Need help?

If you cannot access the app or the in-app delete flow fails, email privacy@skister.app or support@skister.app from the address on your Skister account and ask for account deletion.
